# Welcome to The Entities Ledger Testing Phase
This web application helps track the community-made Hardcore Challenges in Dead by Daylight. Whether you are running a casual gauntlet or a brutal marathon, this tool manages the math, rules, and rosters so you can focus on the trials.

### Supported Challenge Variants
The Ledger currently tracks all major community variants, automating their specific rulesets behind the scenes:
* **Standard:** The classic gauntlet. Track your journey from Ash to Iridescent with standard permadeath rules.
* **Adept:** Restricts your loadouts to only the unique perks of your active killer, locking out add-ons once you reach Bronze.
* **Chaos Shuffle:** Automates re-roll tokens and prevents the use of killers on cooldown while tracking your win streaks.
* **Iron Man:** Enforces the strict 75-minute global timer, manages Mulligan tokens, and ensures no perk or add-on is ever used twice.
* **Blood Money & Afterburn:** Fully integrated economy systems. The app calculates loadout costs, applies dynamic bonuses and penalties (like closed hatch payouts or gen-before-hook fines), and forces roster liquidations if you go bankrupt.

### Core Features
* **Live Roster Management:** As you play, the app tracks the exact status of every killer. Dead, sold, and locked killers are automatically greyed out and removed from the active pool.
* **Dynamic Trial Logging:** Log your survivor outcomes, generator status, and emblem qualities. The app calculates your exact Pip and Grade progression instantly based on current DbD math.
* **Season Analytics:** At the end of a run, review a complete statistical breakdown of your performance, including Kill Rates, Hatch Escapes, Top Perks, and Most Played Killers.
